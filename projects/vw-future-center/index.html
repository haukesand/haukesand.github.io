Volkswagen Group Future Center

Three years of concept prototypes for autonomous and connected vehicles. Self-driving pods, a flying car cockpit, an empathic voice assistant, and driver interfaces.

RoleUX Technologist, Digital User Experience Design, October 2018 to August 2021
WhereVolkswagen Group Future Center Europe
Statusarchived; concept work shown at a high level
Portfolio (PDF) 2021 work portfolio (PDF)

The Future Center is an independent organization in the Volkswagen Group that develops concepts for the group’s many brands. Its UX department had worked on autonomous driving since it was set up, under the vision “mobility for all, at the push of a button”. I joined as a UX Technologist and sat between the strategy, research, and software circles, turning concepts into tangible prototypes that could be tested with users and written down as requirements. Everything below is concept work shown at a high level; the details remain unpublished.

Self-driving pods

The main goal was the user experience of fully autonomous pods, which break with almost everything a car used to be. The relationship to passengers and to the street had to be rethought from the basics. Self-driving cars should give better, more sustainable, and more accessible mobility to everyone, so inclusive design was the starting point. I built the prototypes that verified the concepts with users and turned them into requirements.

Flying cars

A lightweight interface concept for an electric vertical take-off and landing vehicle of a German luxury brand, based on interviews with ultra-high-net-worth individuals. I did the needs analysis, built the hardware for controlling the vehicle, and developed augmented-reality interactions in a flight simulator.

AI voice

An empathic voice assistant for fully and semi-autonomous vehicles, developed with visually impaired and older adults. I trained the models and built the embedded voice recognition (Snips), prototypes with OpenPose, YOLO, and Runway for proactive use cases, and motorized camera shutters for in-cabin privacy. I also tested the voice feedback of autonomous vehicles with visually impaired passengers.

Digital driver UI

Interfaces for connected cars with growing automation that make productivity and entertainment possible on the road. High-fidelity interactive prototypes, fully explorable, integrated with a driving simulator and meant for production. I did the ideation, the front-end software and hardware, and the qualitative and quantitative research.
