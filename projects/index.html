Research tools & sites

2023 – now maintaining
Bright Patterns
Defines the term and collects benevolent design solutions that put user goals and well-being first.
2024 – now maintaining
UEEQ
A five-scale addendum to the UEQ that measures whether users experience a design as deceptive, addictive, coercive, disguised, or annoying.
with Doris Maria Rhomberg
2025 – now recurring
Conscientious Technology Design Workshop
An eight-week summer workshop where Siegel PiTech PhD Impact Fellows surface and design for the values at stake in their public-interest projects.
with Jae June Lee, Helen Nissenbaum
2026 – now just started
Obfuscation.tech
An open repository of obfuscation strategies for people who want to stay autonomous under pervasive surveillance and AI scraping.
with Helen Nissenbaum
Summer 2026 finished
Ethical Vibe Coding
A three-week course for high school students who build real apps with AI coding tools while learning to spot bias, dark patterns, and their own responsibility.
with Jonathan Segal, Wendy Ju
2025 – now paper under review
Human–AI Autonomy Rubric
An evaluation rubric for whether an AI feature undermines, limits, respects, or supports a user's autonomy. Co-designed with 24 experts.

Experiments & installations

2024 – 2025 finished
The Privacy Policy Analysis Canvas
A FigJam canvas that gets students to take a privacy policy apart with contextual integrity, and shows why notice alone cannot protect privacy.
with Helen Nissenbaum
2025 exhibited 2025
App Design as a Commodity
An installation that sells custom apps for Venmo change. A receipt printer takes your order, an AI agent builds and hosts the app, and the receipt carries the QR code.
